Make a bilingual book from any free book

Pick a public-domain book or a Wikipedia article, say which language you're learning, and get ready-made instructions for Claude. Claude translates it sentence by sentence, glosses every word, tags the grammar and adds short teaching notes. The reader page turns the result into pages you can print.

Free books only, please. Project Gutenberg books are public domain in the United States; check your own country's rules too. Wikipedia articles are under CC BY-SA 4.0, so your edition must credit the article and its authors and use the same license; the instructions ask Claude to put that credit in the book. Claude can make mistakes, so treat the notes as a study aid and check anything important.
